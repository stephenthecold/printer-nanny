Quiet hours & maintenance windows

When not to notify. Quiet hours recur weekly and are read in each client's local time — “after 18:00” means the client's 18:00, so one global window covers a fleet across several zones. Maintenance windows are a single dated range in UTC, for planned work. Alerts at or above a window's break-through severity ignore it entirely.
